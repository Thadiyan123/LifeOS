LifeOS

LifeOS is a personal automation tool run by one person, Rahul Krishnan, on their own computer. It isn't a public service, and nobody else can sign in to it.

It uses the YouTube API Services to upload and manage videos on its owner's own YouTube channels and to read those channels' analytics. Every upload is reviewed and approved by the owner before it is published.
